Mirror Bounce

How to play

Place mirrors so the beam passes through all three targets. Tap a cell to cycle / → \ → empty. Each reflection off a mirror is one bounce; match par for full marks. The clock starts with your first mirror.

Keyboard: arrows move, Space cycles, / \ place, Backspace clears, F fires.
